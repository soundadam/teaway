`teaway` keeps a Mac awake and can schedule one shutdown. It cannot manage the room around the machine or the rest of an availability stack.

## Closed-lid thermals

A MacBook has less cooling headroom with its lid closed.

<Warning>
  Never run a MacBook closed inside a bag, backpack, drawer, bedding, or other confined space. Trapped heat can damage the battery, throttle the CPU, or cause a fire. This applies whether teaway is running or not.
</Warning>

Place the machine on a hard, open, well-ventilated surface and keep it stationary before walking away. `teaway` does not override macOS thermal protection.

## Power

Remain on AC power for unattended use. `teaway on` works the same on battery, but a battery can drain silently. Desktop Macs have no battery buffer; a brief mains loss is an immediate shutdown.

## What teaway cannot prevent

Power loss, kernel panic, forced macOS updates, hardware faults, network outages, and thermal shutdown. None of these are teaway failures.

## What teaway does not manage

Remote Login, Screen Sharing, VPNs, firewalls, Wi-Fi, launchd or other supervisors, backups, disk space, or recovery after a crash. Configure those independently, and test them while you still have physical access.

Treat a Mac as a small server only after remote access, service startup, backups, monitoring, stable networking, and—where needed—a UPS are in place.
