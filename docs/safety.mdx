---
title: "Safety"
description: "Closed-lid heat and power."
---

A MacBook cools worse with the lid closed.

<Warning>
  Never run it closed inside a bag, backpack, drawer, or bedding. Trapped heat can wreck the machine.
</Warning>

Leave it on a hard, open surface. Stay on AC if you'll walk away — `teaway` works on battery, but a battery can drain silently.
